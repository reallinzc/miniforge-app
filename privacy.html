隐私说明

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 17 日

作品及其文件保存在设备上，并可通过你自己的 iCloud 私有空间同步。提供商设置和所选 AI 服务也会通过 iCloud 同步。凭据保存在系统钥匙串中，并可随 iCloud 钥匙串同步。

使用 AI 时，请求从设备直接发给你连接并选用的服务商。内容可能包括消息、对话、附件、作品内容、检查截图，以及制作时读取的系统数据。作品自己的 AI 功能也会发送它请求处理的内容；设备端 AI 不可用时，过去的请求还可能用于生成首页建议。连接前会显示数据共享说明。

系统数据访问由系统权限控制。MiniForge 不进行广告跟踪；这不等于第三方服务不会留存请求。服务商按自己的条款处理和留存数据，断开连接不会自动删除其已有记录。

你可在作品库删除作品，删除操作会同步；可在设置中删除服务凭据。云端副本、系统备份及第三方已接收的数据按相应服务的规则处理。

Privacy

Effective November 17, 2026

Creations and their files are stored on your device and can sync through your private iCloud storage. Provider settings and your AI selection also sync through iCloud. Credentials are stored in the system keychain and can sync through iCloud Keychain.

AI requests go directly from your device to the service you connect and select. They may include messages, conversation history, attachments, creation content, check screenshots and system data read during creation. A creation’s own AI features send the content they ask to process. Past requests may also be used for home-screen suggestions when on-device AI is unavailable. A data notice appears before connection.

System permissions control access to system data. MiniForge does not use advertising tracking. This does not establish that external services retain no requests. Each service handles and retains data under its own terms. Disconnecting does not delete data already received by a service.

You can delete creations in the library; deletions sync. You can remove service credentials in Settings. Cloud copies, system backups and data already received by external services follow the relevant service’s rules.
