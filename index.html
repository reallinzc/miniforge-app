使用帮助

用一句话描述想要的作品，AI 为你制作小组件或交互页面。在作品库打开使用，或继续对话调整。

制作前，在设置中连接自己的 AI 服务，阅读并同意数据共享说明。无法连接时，检查网络、凭据和服务可用性；不要把凭据发给支持人员。

添加小组件：长按主屏幕空白处，选择编辑 → 添加小组件 → MiniForge，再长按已添加的小组件选择作品。刷新时间由系统安排。

作品与设置通过自己的 iCloud 同步。同步未完成时，检查系统 iCloud 登录和网络，并在另一台设备查看前等待同步完成。

需要帮助时，请准备应用版本、问题步骤和不含敏感内容的截图。联系：reallinzc@gmail.com

Support

Describe a creation in a sentence. AI makes a widget or interactive page. Open it from the library, or continue the conversation to adjust it.

Connect your own AI service in Settings and agree to the data notice before creating. If connection fails, check your network, credentials and service availability. Never send credentials to support.

To add a widget, hold an empty area of the Home Screen, then choose Edit → Add Widget → MiniForge. Hold the added widget to choose a creation. The system schedules refreshes.

Creations and settings sync through your own iCloud. If sync is pending, check your system iCloud sign-in and network, and wait before opening the creation on another device.

For help, prepare the app version, steps to reproduce and a screenshot without sensitive information. Contact: reallinzc@gmail.com
